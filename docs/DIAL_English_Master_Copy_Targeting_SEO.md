# DIAL Website — Revised English Master Copy, Targeting & SEO

## Purpose

This document replaces the generic “full-service digital agency” positioning with a clearer, more credible DIAL proposition built around the strongest real capabilities behind the business:

- Performance marketing and paid acquisition
- Google Ads and Meta Ads
- Lead generation
- Conversion-focused websites and landing pages
- SEO and search visibility
- GA4, GTM, conversion tracking and attribution
- CRM/source mapping and lead-quality feedback
- Salesforce/Pardot-style lead architecture and automation
- Reporting, measurement and commercial optimisation
- Web development and technical implementation

English is the master language. Translate to Montenegrin and French only after this master is approved.

# 1. Positioning

## Recommended category

**Performance Marketing & Lead Generation Consultancy**

Use “digital marketing agency” selectively in SEO metadata where it matches search intent, but do not make it the main brand identity.

DIAL should not sound like an agency that simply sells websites, social posts, logos and SEO packages.

The stronger position is:

> DIAL connects acquisition, conversion, measurement and CRM so businesses can understand what generates demand, which leads are worth pursuing and where marketing budget should go next.

## Primary audience

DIAL should primarily speak to **lead-driven businesses where each enquiry has meaningful commercial value and lead quality matters more than raw volume**.

Priority audience:

- Luxury real estate and property
- Hospitality, tourism, destination and marina/yachting businesses
- B2B and professional services
- Industrial, engineering and specialist service businesses
- Other high-value businesses with a considered sales cycle

Avoid making the homepage too sector-specific. Sector expertise should be demonstrated through case studies and proof rather than stuffing industries into the hero.

## Geographic positioning

**Based in Montenegro, built for international acquisition.**

Use Montenegro naturally for local SEO and commercial relevance, but do not make DIAL appear limited to Montenegro.

Recommended approach:

- Homepage: Montenegro relevance
- Service pages: Montenegro modifier where commercially useful
- About: mention experience across international markets
- Case studies: naturally establish industry and geographic credibility
- Avoid lists of cities purely for ranking
- Only create location pages when there is enough genuinely useful, unique content for that location

# 2. DIAL voice

The tone should borrow the strongest principles from premium destination/luxury brand writing without copying its vocabulary.

## Clear

Every line should explain either:

- what DIAL does
- why it matters
- what happens next

Avoid filler.

## Confident

State capability plainly.

Avoid:

- “world-class”
- “best”
- “leading”
- “revolutionary”
- “game-changing”
- “outstanding results”

unless independently substantiated.

## Specific

Prefer mechanisms over vague promises.

Weak:

> Data-driven strategies that deliver results.

Strong:

> Connect campaign spend, conversion data and CRM lead quality so budget decisions are based on commercial outcomes.

## Premium through restraint

Do not oversell.

Shorter, more precise copy should carry the brand.

## Experience-led

Show expertise through the actual work:

- paid media
- analytics
- tracking
- CRM
- landing pages
- lead quality
- international market targeting
- reporting

rather than repeatedly saying “expert”, “innovative” or “full-service”.

## English style

Use **UK English** consistently:

- optimise
- organisation
- behaviour
- prioritise

# 3. SEO copy rules used

1. One clear primary search intent per indexable service page.
2. Titles should be unique, concise and descriptive.
3. Use the brand name once in titles, normally at the end.
4. Do not repeat the same keyword unnaturally.
5. H1 should clearly match the page intent without simply copying a keyword list.
6. Write unique meta descriptions that accurately describe the specific page.
7. Search terms should occur naturally in useful copy, not be forced into headings.
8. Use descriptive internal-link anchors rather than generic “Learn more” wherever practical.
9. Demonstrate first-hand expertise and real methods.
10. Avoid location keyword stuffing and blocks of city names.
11. Use case studies and expertise to support credibility instead of unverified claims.
12. Keep each locale genuinely translated and locally natural.
13. Do not create thin pages purely to rank for keyword/location combinations.
14. Service pages should answer buyer intent: problem, approach, scope, proof, next step.
15. Utility pages such as quote forms should normally not compete with core service pages for organic search intent.

# 25. Content principles before translation

Before translating the master:

1. Approve every commercial claim.
2. Remove placeholder clients/testimonials.
3. Replace generic stock proof with genuine work.
4. Confirm whether SEO audit fulfilment actually exists.
5. Confirm consultation duration and no-fee wording.
6. Confirm final service architecture.
7. Decide whether Paid Media and Analytics/CRM get dedicated routes.
8. Decide whether Branding remains a core or supporting service.
9. Confirm target sectors to feature explicitly.
10. Then translate EN → ME and FR naturally.

Do not translate sentence-for-sentence mechanically.

ME and FR should preserve:

- meaning
- hierarchy
- search intent
- tone
- CTA commitment level

not English syntax.
