## Section-to-section rhythm

**Classification: TOO UNIFORM.** The homepage has useful quiet Portfolio and Testimonials passages and a calm Footer, but each service template repeats the same saturated hero, cyan feature icons, popular-plan badge, cyan checkmarks/controls, and saturated closing CTA. The service journey therefore holds a nearly continuous medium/high intensity instead of reserving a clear peak for the principal conversion action.

- The homepage hero is visually dominant through its solid navy field and scale, not through glow—positive.
- About supplies another large navy field, so the page has less tonal relief than the accent count alone suggests.
- Portfolio and Testimonials are the strongest neutral resting points.
- Ordinary service information sections repeatedly activate cyan through every feature icon.
- Closing service CTAs are stronger, but use the same cyan treatment already seen throughout the page.
- Footer closes calmly with restrained white/slate text and a deep navy base.

## Accent-independence test

**Classification: WEAK.** Page headings and large blocks would remain legible without cyan, but service differentiation, process sequencing, package popularity, checkmarks, icon affordance, focus, and several secondary actions all depend on the same accent. Card shadows/borders and typography establish some hierarchy, yet repeated card templates would become visually flat and difficult to prioritise without their cyan cues.

## Primary CTA dominance test

**Book a Strategy Call: VISIBLE BUT COMPETED WITH.** It has a strong cyan fill in navigation and hero contexts, but popular-package buttons, service closing buttons, form next/submit controls, badges, process circles, icon hover fills, the floating mobile control, and outlined secondary actions use the same cyan family at comparable saturation. The CTA remains discoverable by placement and label, not uniquely dominant by colour.

## Hover and motion colour

**Classification: INCONSISTENT / occasionally TOO FLASHY.** Global/nav/hero link hovers are mostly restrained. Service cards combine `shadow-lg → shadow-xl`, cyan icon fill, white icon inversion, and sometimes cyan button inversion in one hover language. Mobile controls additionally switch cyan fills and coloured shadows. Hover usually remains within the brand family, but often changes several material signals at once.
