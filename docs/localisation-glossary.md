# DIAL localisation glossary

This glossary records the terminology used in the approved Montenegrin and French marketing localisation. Product names and acronyms remain unchanged where that is the established professional usage.

| English concept | Montenegrin | French | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Performance Marketing | performance marketing | marketing à la performance | The established English term is retained in ME; the standard professional French equivalent is used in FR. |
| Lead Generation | generisanje leadova | génération de leads | “Lead” remains the most precise professional term in systems/process copy. |
| Paid Media | plaćeni mediji | médias payants | “Plaćene kampanje” / “social ads” may be used where the channel or activity is more specific. |
| Paid Acquisition | plaćena akvizicija | acquisition payante | Refers to acquisition through paid channels, not generic advertising. |
| Qualified Lead | kvalifikovani lead | lead qualifié / prospect qualifié | “Prospect qualifié” is preferred in visible FR propositions; “lead qualifié” is retained in CRM/process contexts. |
| Pipeline | tok kvalifikovanih prodajnih prilika | flux d’opportunités qualifiées | Rendered by commercial meaning rather than as a literal pipe or an unexplained English noun. |
| Conversion | konverzija | conversion | Established marketing terminology. |
| Conversion Tracking | praćenje konverzija | suivi des conversions | Used consistently in measurement copy. |
| Attribution | atribucija | attribution | “Atribucija izvora” / “attribution des sources” where source-level meaning matters. |
| Landing Page | landing stranica | landing page | Established professional terminology; grammar may vary by sentence. |
| Lead Quality | kvalitet leadova | qualité des leads | Refers to downstream commercial relevance, not platform volume. |
| SEO & Search Visibility | SEO i vidljivost u pretrazi | SEO et visibilité dans les moteurs de recherche | SEO is never translated. |
| Organic Search | organska pretraga | recherche organique | “Organska vidljivost” / “visibilité organique” where visibility is the approved concept. |
| Analytics | analitika | analytique | “Analitika” and “analytique” refer to the discipline; product names remain unchanged. |
| Tracking | praćenje | suivi | Technical acronyms such as GA4 and GTM remain unchanged. |
| CRM | CRM | CRM | Never translated. |
| Source Mapping | mapiranje izvora | cartographie des sources | Refers to connecting acquisition sources with lead and CRM data. |
| Strategy Call | strateški razgovor | échange stratégique | Primary CTA: “Zakažite strateški razgovor” / “Réserver un échange stratégique”. This preserves a moderate, non-purchase commitment. |
| Digital Strategy | digitalna strategija | stratégie digitale | “Strategija digitalnog marketinga” / “stratégie de marketing digital” is used where SEO intent is specifically marketing strategy. |
| Web Design & Development | web dizajn i razvoj | conception et développement web | Product and service meaning is preserved without translating route slugs. |
| Brand & Creative Support | podrška za brend i kreativna rješenja | accompagnement de marque et création | Restrained consultancy/support positioning, not a broader creative-agency claim. |
| Analytics, Tracking & CRM | analitika, praćenje i CRM | analytique, suivi et CRM | Service grouping retained as three connected capabilities. |
| Digital Strategy & Consulting | digitalna strategija i konsalting | stratégie digitale et conseil | “Konsalting” is accepted professional ME usage. |
